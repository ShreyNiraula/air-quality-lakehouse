# Check 0.7: city candidates

Run on 6 October 2026 (UTC). The script's unedited output is in `generated/0.7-city-candidates.md`. This page says how to read it and what stands out. Choosing the two cities is the owner's step (Owner B).

## How to read the table

- **A pair** is one reference monitor and one openSenseMap box within 1 km of each other. `plan.md` uses a city only if it has at least three pairs. One box near two monitors counts as two pairs, so the table also gives the number of different boxes and of different monitors.
- **Which monitors count.** OpenAQ lists it as a fixed reference monitor, and its PM2.5 sensor itself first reported on 1 January 2025 or earlier and last reported within 30 days of the run. 63 of the 70 monitors that have a box nearby pass. OpenAQ answered with a server error for the sensor lists of two more, 225714 and 455341, so they are left out.
- **Which boxes count.** The box says `outdoor`, was made on 1 January 2025 or earlier, and has a sensor that the rule from check 0.5b places as PM2.5 and that last reported within 30 days of the run. openSenseMap's list does not say when a sensor first reported, so the box must also have a file of its PM2.5 sensor in the archive on 1 January 2025, the first sample day. 65 of the 70 boxes near a monitor have one. A box that was only offline that day is left out too.
- **A city** is a group of monitors within 20 km of one another, named by the locality OpenAQ gives most often in the group. Where OpenAQ gives no locality, or gives a network's name, the label is a station or network name.
- **Sample days.** For the cities with at least three pairs, the script reads each archive on the first day of each quarter, seven days in all. A monitor counts on a day if its file has PM2.5 rows, a box if its folder has a file of its PM2.5 sensor.

## What stands out

- **47 cities have at least one pair, and 5 have at least three:** Antwerpen, Berlin, Darmstadt, Marburg and Wien.
- **Berlin** has 4 pairs from 4 different boxes and 3 monitors. All have PM2.5 on all 7 sample days, except one box with 6.
- **Wien** has 3 pairs, each with its own monitor and its own box. One box has PM2.5 on 6 of the 7 days.
- **Darmstadt** has 3 pairs, from only 2 boxes and 2 monitors. One box has PM2.5 on 6 of the 7 days.
- **Marburg** has 3 pairs from 2 boxes and 2 monitors. One box, `Marbacher Weg`, has PM2.5 on only 2 of the 7 days. Without it 2 pairs remain, which is below the rule.
- **Antwerpen** has 5 pairs, all with the same single box. Monitor 3313496 has PM2.5 on none of the sample days and carries the same name as monitor 3313497.
- **No monitor in any pair measures temperature or humidity.** The column says `neither` for all 47 cities. `plan.md` already takes the condition value for every pair from Open-Meteo weather at the monitor's location, and uses the box's own humidity as a second view, so this changes nothing in the plan.

## What this does not show

- **The 70% rule.** `plan.md` compares a pair only if the two share at least 70% of hours. That needs the readings themselves, so it is checked once a city's data is loaded. Seven sample days are only a first filter.
- **Boxes the name rule misses.** Check 0.5b showed the rule misses some PM2.5 sensors with unusual names, so a city may have a few more boxes than listed here.
