# Check 0.5b: openSenseMap

Run on 5 October 2026. openSenseMap replaces Sensor.Community as the low-cost source. The script's unedited output is in `generated/0.5b-opensensemap.md` (the newest day, 4 October 2026) and `generated/0.5b-opensensemap-2025-01-01.md` (the first day the plan loads). This page adds what was read by eye and what the findings mean. Two findings differ from `plan.md`; neither stops the source being used.

## Licence: clear, with no conditions

- **What openSenseMap states.** Its own site text (<https://opensensemap.org/translations/en_US.json>) says "All data is licensed under Public Domain Dedication and License 1.0 and free to use." A person registering a box agrees "that the sensor data you submit can be freely used by the public" under that licence. The script reads both. The archive listing and the files themselves carry no licence text.
- **What the licence says**, read by eye at <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1-0/>:
  - 3.1: "The Rightsholder by using this Document, dedicates the Work to the public domain for the benefit of the public and relinquishes all rights in Copyright and Database Rights over the Work."
  - 3.3a, for places where a dedication is not possible: "The Licensor grants to You a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, licence to Use the Work [...] These rights explicitly include commercial use, and do not exclude any field of endeavour."
  - No clause asks for a credit or for share-alike. Section 5 gives the data "as is", without warranty.
- **Permissions, for the source manifest.** Redistribution, modification and commercial use are allowed, by 3.1 and 3.3a. Attribution is not required. Share-alike is not required.

## What differs from `plan.md`

- **16 days from 1 January 2025 to 3 October 2026 have no folder.** The output lists them; five are in November 2025, the 15th to the 18th and the 24th. `plan.md`'s facts table says the listing has a folder for every day, which is wrong. The backfill must treat a missing folder as "no file", not as an error.
- **Faulty temperatures exist.** On 1 January 2025, 31 of 9,890 sampled temperature readings are outside -60 to 60, all from one sensor, from -146.8 to 182.1 °C. On 4 October 2026 none is. Humidity has none outside 0 to 100 on either day. This supports keeping both proposed ranges.

## What the registry and the contract need to know

- **One box, one folder, one file per sensor.** The three sensor files of the box read each have the header `createdAt,value`, timestamps ending in `Z`, and a reading about every 2.5 minutes. The box id joins the sensors.
- **The timestamps are UTC.** Checked by hand, not by the script: at 16:44:36 UTC on 5 October 2026, <https://api.opensensemap.org/boxes/5ac38bcf850005001bfd2559> gave the box's newest reading as `2026-10-05T16:44:26.034Z`, ten seconds earlier.
- **A sensor's name and unit are typed by its owner**, mostly in German: `Temperatur`, `rel. Luftfeuchte`, `Feinstaub PM2,5`. The script places a sensor by name and unit together, leaves out names that say ground or water, and lists every sensor in these units that it skips. The lists show why no rule on names is safe: it missed a real PM2.5 sensor, named `Feinstaub 2,5um`, and soil sensors (`Bodentemperatur`, `Bodenfeuchte`, `Temperatur (Hochbeet)`) and dew-point sensors (`Taupunkt`, `Dewpoint`) share the units of air temperature and humidity. So for the chosen boxes the registry must list each sensor id by hand.
- **Not every box is usable.** 2 of the 20 sampled on 4 October 2026 say `indoor`, so task 0.7 must keep outdoor boxes only. By the script's rule, 15 of those 20, and 18 of 20 on 1 January 2025, have PM2.5, temperature and humidity. A box can carry several sensors of one kind, such as `Temperatur` and `Temperatur (HECA)`: the 20 boxes of 4 October 2026 hold 24 temperature sensors, and 3 of them sent no reading that day.
- **A day's folder is last modified 8.2 hours after the UTC day ends**, on each of the newest 30 days. That is the last write, not the first.
